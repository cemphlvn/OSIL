# Licensing — MIT (ratified 2026-08-18, superseding same-day Apache-2.0)

**MIT for the entire repository**, with **DCO sign-off** (`git commit -s`)
retained for contributions (DCO is license-independent). No CLA.

History and rationale:
- 2026-08-18 (earlier): Apache-2.0 + DCO ratified at open-sourcing, following
  research U1/U2 (LF AI & Data norms).
- 2026-08-18 (this change): maintainer relicensed to MIT "for now". Clean to
  do: the maintainer is the sole copyright holder (verified: all 48 commits
  share one identity; no external contributions had landed). MIT is
  OSI-approved, so the LF AI & Data Sandbox path is unaffected.
- Known trade-off, recorded honestly: MIT carries NO explicit patent grant,
  which Apache-2.0 did. For a specification project this matters more as
  adoption grows. Revisiting Apache-2.0 (or a spec-track license, see
  docs/research/U2-license-pairing.md) before foundation submission remains
  open; any future relicense requires ratification and, once external
  contributors exist, their consent.
