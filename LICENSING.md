# Licensing — RATIFIED 2026-08-18

**Apache-2.0 for the entire repository** (spec prose, grammar, conformance
corpus, tools), with **DCO sign-off** (`git commit -s`) for contributions.
No CLA.

Rationale (maintainer ratification, 2026-08-18, on U1/U2 research): one
OSI-approved license satisfies the LF AI & Data Sandbox requirement and
matches that foundation's proven spec-only precedent (Bitol/ODCS) and ONNX
itself. U2's alternative (Community Specification License 1.0 for spec/) is
recorded in docs/research/U2-license-pairing.md and can be revisited if the
JDF/standards track is ever chosen instead — that decision would be a
ratified change, not a drift.

History: this file blocked external contributions from G0 until ratification,
by design.
