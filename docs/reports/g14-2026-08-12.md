## Honesty notes
- **The realizability finding is a real vocabulary/machinery gap, now half
  closed.** `<=>` is bidirectional as assertion but directed as computation
  for 3 of 6 fixtures. spec/interop/egraph.md documents the adapter rule, but
  `spec/core.md`'s equivalence definition does not yet mention realizability
  — flagged for a univocity-lint pass, not silently patched into core prose.
- **Guard facts are literal, not reasoned over.** `regime__ExactArithmetic`
  and `numeric_semantics__exact` are DISTINCT facts; ADR-0007's declared
  expansion (ExactArithmetic ≡ numeric_semantics = exact) is not computed
  over. Regime-expansion reasoning is future machinery, deliberately out of
  G14's scope.
- **Scope is narrow and declared:** arithmetic SIR terms, 1-rule-per-lane
  systems, saturation fixed at 5 steps (no termination argument for larger
  rule sets — U5 flags engine performance at scale as un-researched).
  Fixture 008 (n-way `equivalent_under`) is NOT in the suite — binary
  equivalences only; the suite grows monotonically from here.
- **Extraction cost model is the engine default** (ast size); the contract
  does not yet declare a cost semantics. The distributivity extraction
  choosing the factored form is default-cost behavior, not a contracted
  guarantee.
- **Witness diversity (GOVERNANCE SHOULD):** U5's independent execution of
  fixture 003 is a second lane but the same model family ran it. The
  foreign-witness lane for G14's closure claim is the maintainer reading this
  report + the naming ratification still owed on ADR-0009.
- **Matrix wart:** onnx_roundtrip.py rewrites matrix.yaml wholesale; the
  egraph harness preserves foreign cells. A standalone `just roundtrip`
  therefore transiently drops the egraph cell until the next `just egraph`
  (self-healing within any full `just test`). Follow-up belongs to
  matrix-refresh; G3 tooling left untouched by design.
