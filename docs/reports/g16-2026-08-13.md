## Honesty notes
- **The view surfaced a real gap it did not invent**: Identity's contract
  fields are declared (preserves {semantics, visual_layout}, empty
  may_lose) but carry NO mechanical verification — the view honestly
  renders it "uncontracted" alongside MLIR. A G3-style identity round-trip
  harness is the obvious closer; until then the spec's "unique empty
  may_lose" claim is prose, not evidence.
- **views inherits the matrix wart's transient**: it reads matrix.yaml for
  scores, so a standalone `just roundtrip` (which clobbers the egglog
  cell) makes a standalone `just views` fail until the next full
  `just test`. This is the THIRD motivating case for the per-adapter-cell
  refactor behind ES004 — the backlog item is now priced by three
  independent annoyances.
- **Read-write ordering, again**: views must run after roundtrip/egraph to
  see fresh scores — declared in its read-set, invisible to the
  write-write guard. The Bernstein refinement (ADR-0010) accumulates its
  second and third motivating cases; still unimplemented, still
  separately ratifiable.
- **Geometry constants are ungoverned by design**: box sizes and gaps live
  in the renderer; changing them changes SVGs (advisory) but not data
  (gate). The gate is on meaning; aesthetics remain austere and D1/D2
  remain open.
- **The atlas remains outside**: the claude.ai artifact is presentation;
  its two central diagrams now have governed in-repo counterparts, which
  is the honest division ADR-0011 drew.
- **Witness diversity**: same model family throughout; the mechanical
  hedges are the two in-run witnesses and the zero-diff gate — the
  foreign-witness lane is the maintainer reading this report.
